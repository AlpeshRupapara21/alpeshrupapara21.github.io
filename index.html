Scope, honestly

What I can do — and what I can't

Every engineer has a boundary. Knowing mine is why the things inside it are reliable. I would rather tell you this now than discover it together in month three.

Bring me in for this

  • Architecting a native iOS app that has to last years, not a sprint
  • Swift Concurrency done correctly — actors, isolation, Sendable, data-race safety
  • Diagnosing what profilers show but logs do not: memory, crashes, failed connections
  • Leading a mobile team and setting the review, QA and release process around it
  • Owning mobile and backend as one system rather than two contracts
  • Release engineering — signing, provisioning, Fastlane, TestFlight, store review
  • Designing SDKs other developers have to integrate without reading the source
  • Building governed AI development pipelines with real review and security gates

Hire someone else for this

  • Brand and visual identity — I build faithfully to a design system, I don't originate one
  • Training or researching ML models — I integrate and ship them on-device, that's the line
  • Distributed systems at planet scale — my backend work is product scale, and I'll say so
  • Native Android as the deepest specialism — I lead and review it; a dedicated Android engineer goes further
  • Game engines — Unity and Unreal are not mine; my game shipped on React Native
  • Embedded firmware — I talk to hardware over BLE, I don't write what runs on it
